## Failed fixtures and corrections

Initial runs intentionally remain in the local evidence bundle:

1. Follow resumed from less than the ten-block start threshold. Move the owner far enough to request following; both follow directions now pass.
2. Quick Healer's fixture set health to 40, the maximum, leaving nothing to heal. Start at 20; after 80 ticks level 5 heals while level 4 remains at 20. The intended assertion is unchanged: compare healing at the seated level boundary.
3. Fishing initially disabled AI and asserted after a fixed delay. Enable normal AI with a sit order; the later diagnostic showed shaking still in progress at tick 80. Await actual fishing loot within the 400-tick deadline instead of declaring failure mid-shake. Pin the success branch to avoid chance-based CI failures.
4. An intermediate diagnostic referenced a nonexistent public shake accessor and failed compilation. Correct it to the actual `getDogClassicalShakeAnim` API; that run is not gameplay evidence.

No production defect was reproduced in these scenarios. Concrete diff review checked fixture isolation, RNG, outcome assertions, resource casing, cleanup and retained fork behavior. Test entities are discarded on success and their location/revival entries removed; the isolated world is stopped/disposed after failed runs.
